# FRAME visual direction

Editorial cinematic, confirmed by the user. Replace the initial dark-blue, coral, slogan-led website completely.

Warm paper (#f3f0e8), near-black ink (#191b18), olive-grey (#62675b), signal chartreuse (#daf564). Cinematic deep greens and warm amber belong to film photography. Use contrast-checked ink for text on paper and chartreuse, paper for text on dark grounds. Muted labels must remain readable.

Expressive self-hosted serif display type paired with a crisp neutral sans for navigation, forms and data. Type contrast and composition establish hierarchy; avoid eyebrows above every heading. No gradient text, decorative dashboard metrics, pill-card grids, pseudo-reviews, fake trailers or generated core UI text.

The home page opens on an oversized editorial masthead and a real featured film composition. The film itself leads: panoramic photographic image, title, truthful metadata, distinct Book tickets control and a manually operated feature selector. Below: real film listings, genre/language browsing, immediate showtimes. Warm margins and strong image crops; quiet hairline divisions instead of nested cards.

Film details: confident poster/image and readable narrative, quick date strip and grouped showtimes with prices and availability. Booking: theatre-like spatial seat map plus paper ticket summary; unmistakable selection, held/booked states beyond colour, countdown with numeric precision, honest simulated-payment controls. Confirmation feels like a cinema ticket, with no decorative fake scannable QR code.

One coherent motion system: short film-change image/caption transition, pointer-responsive poster depth, spatial selection feedback, ticket reveal after confirmation. Controls always work without motion. Respect reduced motion. Sound defaults off; explicit accessible toggle enables quiet seat tick and confirmation chime via Web Audio, no background autoplay.

All flows retain accessible labels, focus management, error recovery, ownership gates, network state and refresh recovery. Mobile seat map stays readable/tappable with intentional scroll container rather than shrinking targets to fit. No horizontal page overflow at375/768/1440px. Keyboard navigation is first-class.
