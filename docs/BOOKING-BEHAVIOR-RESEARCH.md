## Design inferences (not provider-documented behavior)

- A booking product should treat confirmation plus the user-visible order record as the success boundary, especially after an ambiguous payment result. This is an inference from the providers’ recovery instructions, not proof of any provider’s payment state machine.
- Do not copy a seat-hold countdown or release policy from these sources: none of the reviewed official documents specifies one.
- Fees should be visible before a customer commits payment. District documents platform/confirmation-screen fee communication, while Fandango documents fee inclusion and Cinemark documents an online fee; the precise UI timing is only documented by District in the reviewed materials.
